# Productietoegang aanvragen (Play Console)

Na 14 dagen gesloten testen met minstens 12 testers verschijnt in de Play Console op het **Dashboard** de knop **Productietoegang aanvragen**. Dan krijg je een formulier met de 10 vragen hieronder. Google beoordeelt je aanvraag daarna (meestal binnen een week). Pas als die is goedgekeurd, kun je een release naar **Productie** sturen en staat De Spelkeet voor iedereen in de Play Store.

**Doe dit vóór je het formulier invult:**
1. Zet de nieuwe teksten en de 8 nieuwe screenshots uit deze map (`teksten.md`, `screenshot-1…8`) bij *Hoofdvermelding in de Store*. In vraag 8 zeg je dat je dat hebt gedaan, dus het moet ook echt zo zijn.
2. Check of je gesloten test echt 14 dagen liep met minstens 12 testers die al die tijd aangemeld bleven.

**Over de antwoorden:** Testers Community gaf voorbeeldantwoorden. Hieronder staan die, maar dan aangepast aan wat er echt is gebeurd: de verbeteringen zijn al gedaan (niet "we gaan het doen"), en de echte spellen en functies staan erin. Antwoord in het Engels; dat leest Google het snelst. Kopieer wat in de grijze blokken staat. Klopt iets niet (bijvoorbeeld hoe je testers vond), pas het dan aan: eerlijk is belangrijker dan mooi.

### 1. How did you recruit users for your closed test?

```
We used a paid testing provider (Testers Community) to get a broad group of testers on different devices and Android versions. We also shared the app with friends and our target group: people who play card and party games together at gatherings, so we also got feedback from real game nights.
```
*Heb je het niet aan vrienden gegeven? Haal dan de tweede zin weg.*

### 2. How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?

Kies: **Easy** (Makkelijk)

### 3. Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test

```
Testers played the games together on their own phones, created tables with a code and joined each other's games. They tried the multiplayer card games, dice games and the one-phone games, and used the in-app review form after finishing a game. They reported no crashes or bugs, and gave concrete suggestions about onboarding, sharing, rating and our store listing, which we used to improve the app.
```

### 4. Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers. Include how you collected the feedback.

```
Testers reported that the app was stable on all tested devices and Android versions, with no crashes or critical bugs. Their suggestions were: a walkthrough for new users, a better store description with relevant keywords, more informative screenshots, and options to share and rate the app. We collected the feedback through a written test report from the testing provider, the in-app review form that appears after a game (stars plus "what went well" and "what went wrong"), and the in-app "Report something" page.
```

### 5. Who is the intended audience for your app?

```
Adults and young adults (students, friends, families at parties) who want to play social card, dice and party games together when they meet up. Each player uses their own phone, or the group shares one phone on the table. The app is in Dutch and aimed at players in the Netherlands and Belgium.
```

### 6. Describe how your app provides value to the users.

```
De Spelkeet bundles 16 party games in one app, such as Bussen, Mexen, Toepen, Kingsen, Arena (duels with 20 mini-games) and a racing game. Friends play together in real time on their own phones: one person creates a table and shares a four-letter code, the rest joins. You don't need cards, dice, an account or a sign-up, and there are no ads. Every game has a short visual tutorial, so a group can start playing within a minute.
```

### 7. How many installs do you expect your app to have in your first year?

Kies wat je zelf realistisch vindt. Een eerlijke inschatting voor een Nederlandstalige app zonder reclamebudget is **1K – 10K**. Wil je actief promoten (TikTok, studentenverenigingen), dan kun je **10K – 100K** kiezen.

### 8. What changes did you make to your app based on what you learned during your closed test?

```
Based on the feedback we made these changes:
- A walkthrough for new users on the home screen (can be skipped and restarted from the menu), plus a one-time tip in every game to open the visual tutorial.
- A "Share De Spelkeet" option in the menu with a ready-made message and links.
- A "Rate us" option in the menu that opens the Play Store. After a positive in-app review we ask once, with a clear "No thanks", if the player also wants to rate us on the Play Store.
- A "Give feedback" option in the menu.
- A rewritten store description with relevant keywords and all games, and 8 new screenshots that show real gameplay with a short caption.
- Automatic error reporting, so we see problems on players' phones and can fix them quickly.
We also added new content during the test, such as the game Arena with 20 mini-games.
```

### 9. How did you decide that your app is ready for production?

```
The app ran stable during the closed test on many different devices and Android versions, without crashes or critical bugs. All games were tested with several players at the same time, and we have automated tests for the game rules and the server. We implemented the improvements our testers asked for, and new players now get a walkthrough and a tutorial per game. With error reporting in place, we can follow up on issues quickly after launch.
```

### 10. What did you do differently this time?

```
This is our first app in the Play Store. Compared to our first version, we tested much more broadly: on many devices through a testing provider and with real groups of players, and we collected feedback inside the app after every game. We acted on that feedback before going to production, with a focus on helping new players get started and on making the app easier to find and share.
```
